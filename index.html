About

The artist behind XAQI arts

Saqib Maqbool Khan is an AI Generation Artist and creative media specialist based in Wah Cantt, Pakistan. His pipeline spans generative AI, prompt engineering and digital video post-production — from hyper-realistic AI spokespersons and UGC ad creatives to cinematic AI trailers and series.

Before AI, there was the hand: years of charcoal portraiture, impasto oil painting, murals and Arabic calligraphy. That foundation is why his AI work looks directed, not generated.
